Why Winter Lawn Care Is So Important

By Brandon McClellan, Central Texas Lawn Leveling

It can be easy to forget about lawn care during the winter months. After all, when it's cold, who wants to be outside when they can be inside in the warmth? However, lawn care in the winter is just as important as lawn care in the summer — even here in Central Texas. In this post from our team at CTXLL, we'll discuss why winter lawn care is so important. Learn more below and contact us today for winter lawn care services in Central Texas!

Prevent Pests

Just because your grass isn't growing, doesn't mean that pests are taking a break. In fact, many pests are more active in the winter months, especially ones that burrow. With winter lawn care, you can help prevent pests from taking over your dormant lawn and causing damage.

Help Your Lawn Recover

After a long, hot Texas summer, your lawn needs all the help it can get to recover and be ready for next year. The hot summer sun can take a toll on your grass, leaving it yellow and patchy. Winter is the perfect time to give your lawn a little extra TLC in the form of lawn care.

Avoid Future Problems

By taking care of your lawn in the winter, you can help to avoid problems down the road. For example, if you don't clear away debris, it can kill the grass underneath. Or, if you don't aerate your lawn, compacted soil can lead to a host of problems like thatch buildup and poor drainage.

Get A Head Start On Spring

Finally, by taking care of your lawn in the winter, you can get a head start on spring growth. By clearing dead leaves and other debris, fertilizing, and aerating your lawn in the winter, you'll give it a head start come spring.
