What Is Pre-Emergent And Why Your Lawn Needs It This Fall

By Brandon McClellan, Central Texas Lawn Leveling

Pre-emergent herbicides are a key part of any lawn care program. They can prevent many common weeds from ever taking hold in your yard.

We use pre-emergent herbicides as part of our lawn care services and recommend them to our clients because they effectively control weeds.

Before using pre-emergent herbicides, you should know a few things. This blog post will help you better understand what pre-emergent is and why you need it for your lawn. Schedule an appointment with us today!

What Is Pre-Emergent?

Pre-emergent herbicides are applied to the soil before weeds start to grow. They work by creating a barrier that prevents weed seeds from germinating.

Why Is Pre-Emergent Important?

Pre-emergent is important because it can prevent weeds and avoid the need to use herbicides later on. Furthermore, it can help you maintain a healthy lawn.

When Should You Use Pre-Emergent?

The best time to use pre-emergent is in the fall, before winter sets in. This is because many weed seeds germinate in the spring. By applying pre-emergent in the fall, you can prevent these weeds from ever taking hold.

How Do You Use Pre-Emergent?

Applying pre-emergent is relatively simple. You can either spread it by hand or use a spreader. If you are using a spreader, make sure to calibrate it before use. This will ensure that you apply the herbicide evenly.
