What is the LCRA Rebate Program?

The Lower Colorado River Authority's rebate program is designed to encourage water conservation by promoting water-efficient landscaping practices. By adopting these practices, homeowners can reduce their water usage, save on their water bills, and contribute to the overall health of our local water resources.

How Central Texas Lawn Leveling Can Help You Qualify

Topdressing is an efficient way to increase water retention, which helps increase water savings for your lawn. Here's how our services at Central Texas Lawn Leveling can help you qualify for the LCRA rebate:

  • Improved Irrigation Efficiency: A level lawn ensures that water is distributed evenly, reducing runoff and promoting better absorption. This means your lawn will require less water to stay green and healthy.
  • Enhanced Soil Health: Core Aeration & Topdressing to your lawn can improve soil structure and promote deeper root growth. Healthy soil retains moisture better, reducing the need for frequent watering.

Steps to Claim Your Rebate

  1. Check Eligibility: Visit the LCRA website to review the rebate program's eligibility criteria. Make sure your property qualifies and that you meet all the requirements.
  2. Schedule a Consultation: Contact Central Texas Lawn Leveling to schedule a consultation. Our experts will assess your lawn and determine the best approach to maximize your water efficiency.
  3. Complete the Application: Once your Core Aeration & Topdressing project is completed, fill out the LCRA rebate application. Be sure to include all required documentation, such as receipts and proof of completion.
  4. Submit Your Application: Submit your completed application to the LCRA. If approved, you could receive a rebate of up to $600 to offset the cost of your Core Aeration & Topdressing project.
