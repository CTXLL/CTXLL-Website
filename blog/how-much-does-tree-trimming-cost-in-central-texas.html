How Much Does Tree Trimming Cost In Central Texas?

By Brandon McClellan, Central Texas Lawn Leveling · August 13, 2025

Quick answer

In Central Texas, trimming a small tree (under 10 feet) typically costs $100–$175 and a medium tree (12 to 18 feet) $200–$350. Debris haul-off can add $50–$100 with some companies; we include basic haul-off.

If you’re a homeowner in Central Texas—especially with a home that’s 5 to 18 years old—you’ve probably noticed your trees filling out and reaching new heights. Regular trimming not only boosts curb appeal but also protects your home from storm damage and overgrowth. But how much should you expect to pay?

Here’s everything you need to know when looking for affordable tree services near me, including real pricing and smart ways to save.

Average Tree Trimming Prices by Tree Size:

Tree size and accessibility are two of the biggest factors that determine price. Here’s a general breakdown of average trimming costs in Central Texas:

Small Trees (Under 10 feet)

Cost: $100 – $175

These are typically younger ornamental trees or newly planted shade trees. Most can be trimmed from the ground with minimal equipment.

Medium Trees (12 to 18 feet)

Cost: $200 – $350

These are the most common in homes 5–18 years old. They may require ladders or pole saws, and more time to shape or thin out.

Note: We do not currently service large trees (over 18 feet). For safety and quality, we focus on trees that can be trimmed efficiently from the ground or with light equipment.

Hidden Costs to Watch Out For:

Some companies offer a low base price, but then surprise you with extra charges. Be sure to ask about:

  • Debris Haul-Off: Removing limbs and brush after trimming can add $50–$100, depending on volume. We include basic haul-off in our pricing.
  • Permit Requirements: If you live in areas like Austin, Round Rock, or Cedar Park, certain trees may be protected and require permits to trim.
  • Obstructed Access: Trees over fences, next to structures, or near power lines may have extra fees due to safety precautions or equipment needs.

Cost-Saving Tips for Tree Trimming in Central Texas

You don’t have to sacrifice quality to find affordable tree services near me. Here are ways homeowners in Central Texas are saving on routine tree care:

  • Bundle and Save: Combine services like shrub trimming, brush removal, or light landscaping with your tree trim for a discount.
  • Ask About HOA or Neighborhood Discounts: If your neighborhood has multiple homes needing tree work, we can often trim several homes in one visit—saving everyone money.
  • Trim in the Off-Season: Late fall and winter are the slowest months for most tree companies. Booking then can lead to lower rates and quicker service.
