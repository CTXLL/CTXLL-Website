What Sand And Organic Compost Material Should I Use To Level?

By Brandon McClellan, Central Texas Lawn Leveling

Are you considering using sand to level your lawn but unsure which material to choose? We understand that every yard is unique and requires a tailored approach. Our team does not blindly quote jobs without first examining any underlying issues that may be present.

At Central Texas Lawn Leveling, we have been using straight masonry sand for many years with great success. However, we recently switched to a custom 80/20 blend of sand and high-organic matter compost to produce a topdressing mix that provides superior leveling qualities to sand and improves water and nutrient retention.

While straight masonry sand can cause drying out in hot temperatures, our custom topdressing blend provides additional water retention, which helps your grass recover as the summer heat continues. Not only have we made this important change without raising prices, but we also feel it is in the best interest of our clients. We offer it at a competitive price range equivalent to what 100% Masonry Sand currently sells for.

Just as a new seed needs ample water to produce, top dressing is the key product to cover the seed and hold water. With our custom topdressing blend, we believe we have found the perfect solution for leveling your lawn. Let us help you get the lawn you've always wanted.
