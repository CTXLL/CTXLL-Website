CENTRAL TEXAS LAWN LEVELING PRIVACY POLICY

1. INTRODUCTION

At Central Texas Lawn Leveling ("Company," "we," "us," or "our"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information we may collect from you or that you may provide when you visit our website at https://centraltexaslawnleveling.com (our "Website") and our practices for collecting, using, maintaining, protecting, and disclosing that information.

2. SCOPE OF THIS POLICY

This Privacy Policy applies to information we collect:

  • On this Website.
  • In email, text messages, and other electronic messages between you and this Website.
  • Through mobile and desktop applications you download from this Website.
  • When you interact with our advertising and applications on third-party websites and services, if those applications or advertising include links to this policy.

This policy does NOT apply to information collected:

  • By us offline or through any other means.
  • By any third party, including through any application or content that may link to or be accessible from the Website.

By accessing or using this Website, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

3. CHILDREN UNDER THE AGE OF 13

Our Website is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected such information without parental consent, we will delete it.

4. INFORMATION WE COLLECT ABOUT YOU

We may collect:

Personal Information

Name, address, email address, phone number, and other identifiers.

Non-Personal Information

Information that does not directly identify you.

Technical Information

IP address, browser type, operating system, usage data, and device information.

5. HOW WE COLLECT INFORMATION

We collect information:

  • Directly from you
  • Automatically through cookies and tracking technologies
  • From service providers assisting in operating our Website

When you provide your phone number, we may use it to:

  • Send appointment reminders

You may opt out of marketing SMS messages by texting:

We retain phone numbers and consent records as required by law or while you maintain a relationship with us.
