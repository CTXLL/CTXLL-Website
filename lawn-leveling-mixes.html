Mix questions

What is the best mix for new sod?

One with compost in it. New sod usually sits on soil that hasn't had time to build up organic matter. Compost isn't required, but building organic matter saves on water and fertilizer and helps the lawn through drought over the long run.

Can I level my lawn with just compost?

Not really. Compost-heavy blends like our 80/20 build soil but have little leveling ability. To fill low spots you need more sand, like our 50/50 for some dips or our 80/20 leveling blend (80% sand) to level.

How much topdressing do I need per 1,000 sq ft?

Topdressing goes down 1/8" to 1/3", which at the top end is about 1 cubic yard per 1,000 sq ft.

How much leveling mix do I need per 1,000 sq ft?

About 1.5 cubic yards for a 1/2" pass, and about 2 cubic yards for a more aggressive 2/3" pass. The lighter pass recovers faster.
