The short answer

What is lawn leveling?

Lawn leveling, also called lawn smoothing, fills low spots and dips in an established lawn with a sand-and-compost mix, usually right after core aeration. The grass grows up through the mix, leaving a smoother surface you can mow lower. Most Central Texas lawns need 1 to 3 sessions depending on how uneven they are.

Why our mix is different

Compost blends built for Central Texas soil

The median organic matter across our Central Texas soil tests is 2.71%, which is low. Straight sand smooths a yard but does nothing for that. Our 80/20 blend (80% compost, 20% sand) builds organic matter fastest, for a healthier lawn that holds moisture and handles drought better. Our 50/50 blend goes down a little heavier, to help with some dips while still adding compost. For leveling we flip it: 80% sand, 20% compost.

Screened, manure-based compost with no bio-solids, no sticks, rocks, glass or plastic, plus coffee grounds for slow-release nitrogen.

Lawn leveling & topdressing questions

How much does lawn leveling cost in Central Texas?

Lawn leveling has an $1,800 minimum, and compost topdressing has a $750 minimum. From there, the price depends on the area, the lawn size and how thick the material goes down. Call for a quote on your yard. See pricing

When is the best time to level a lawn in Texas?

During the active growing season, once soil temperatures have the grass growing, because the grass has to grow up through the mix. Around Austin that's generally mid-March through October.

How often should I topdress my lawn?

Once or twice a year. Twice a year, in spring and fall, builds organic matter faster. Increasing organic matter helps the yard hold more water. A soil test shows how much organic matter you're gaining over time.

Should I level with sand or compost?

Sand gives you the level surface; compost adds the organic matter most Central Texas soil is missing. Compost isn't required, but building organic matter saves on water and fertilizer and helps the lawn through drought over the long run. That's why we mix it into our leveling material.

How many sessions will my yard need?

Usually 1 to 3, depending on how uneven it is.

Does lawn leveling or topdressing qualify for an LCRA rebate?

Yes. The LCRA rebate is still running. Homeowners can get up to $1,200, and commercial properties such as city parks, soccer fields and sports fields can get up to $5,000. Eligibility and the application are set by LCRA; we'll walk you through what you need from us, like the invoice and proof of completion.

Is lawn leveling the same as grading?

No. Grading shapes raw ground before sod is laid. Leveling, also called lawn smoothing, smooths an established lawn after the sod is down. Leveling vs. grading explained

How fast can I get a quote?

Usually right on the phone. We can give a rough estimate based on property size, location, material type, the services you want, accessibility and a few other details, then confirm it with a written quote.
