Tree trimming questions

How much does tree trimming cost in Central Texas?

Small trees (under 10 feet) typically run $100–$175 and medium trees (12 to 18 feet) $200–$350. We do not currently service trees over 18 feet. Full cost guide

When is the best time to trim trees in Central Texas?

Generally late winter to early spring, typically January through March. Avoid trimming live oaks between February and June because of oak wilt risk. Timing guide
