Core Aeration in Austin, Georgetown & Round Rock

Core aeration pulls small plugs of soil out of your lawn so air, water and nutrients reach the roots. In compacted Central Texas clay, it's the step that makes leveling and topdressing actually work into the soil.

Aeration FAQ

When is the best time to aerate in Central Texas?

Anytime between February and October, while the grass is actively growing.

How often should I aerate?

It depends on how compacted the soil is, but at least once a year. Heavily compacted clay can benefit from aerating in both spring and fall.

Do you leave the cores on the lawn?

Yes. The cores break down on their own. You only need to remove them if you reel mow.

Can you aerate new sod?

Once it's established. Same rule as leveling: the lawn needs to be rooted in first.

Does core aeration qualify for the LCRA rebate?

Yes. The LCRA rebate is still running. Homeowners can get up to $1,200, and commercial properties such as city parks, soccer fields and sports fields can get up to $5,000. Eligibility and the application are set by LCRA; we'll walk you through what you need from us, like the invoice and proof of completion.
