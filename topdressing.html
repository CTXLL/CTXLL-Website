Compost Topdressing in Austin, Georgetown & Round Rock

Topdressing spreads a thin, even layer of screened compost or a sand-and-compost blend over your lawn. It builds organic matter in thin Central Texas soil, breaks down thatch and helps the yard hold water, without burying the grass.

Why it matters here

Central Texas soil is short on organic matter

The median organic matter across our Central Texas soil tests is 2.71%. Healthy turf does best around 4–5%. Topdressing with compost is how you close that gap, a little every season. Increasing the organic matter in a yard helps it retain more water.

Kansas State Research and Extension estimates each 1% increase in organic matter can mean up to 25,000 more gallons of available soil water per acre.

Topdressing or leveling?

Topdressing is a layer of our 80/20 (80% compost, 20% sand) across the whole lawn to build organic matter, or our 50/50 when the yard also has some dips. Leveling is a separate service: much more material, our 80% sand leveling blend, worked into the low spots to make the surface flat.

Want healthier soil that holds water? Topdress. Want the yard flat so you can mow low? Level.

Topdressing FAQ

How often should I topdress my lawn?

Once or twice a year. Twice a year builds organic matter faster. A soil test shows how much organic matter you're gaining over time, so you can see it working.

When is the best time to topdress in Texas?

Most of our services need to be done during the active growing season. Our spring and fall topdressing program starts in about February and wraps up in October.

How much does topdressing cost?

Topdressing has a $750 minimum. From there, the price depends on the area, the lawn size and how thick the compost goes down. Call and we can usually give you a rough number on the phone. See pricing

How thick is topdressing?

Between 1/8" and 1/3", which at the top end is about 1 cubic yard per 1,000 sq ft. Anything heavier is leveling.

Which blend should I topdress with?

Our 80/20 (80% compost, 20% sand) pushes the most compost, to build organic matter so the yard is healthier and holds up better in drought or when water is short. If the yard also has some dips, our 50/50 goes down a little heavier to help with those while still adding compost. If the yard needs to be flat, that is leveling, with our 80% sand leveling blend. Compare blends

Can you topdress St. Augustine or a shaded lawn?

Yes. We'll topdress any lawn, St. Augustine and shaded yards included. (We don't level shaded yards, but we can topdress them.)

Does topdressing qualify for the LCRA rebate?

Yes. The LCRA rebate is still running. Homeowners can get up to $1,200, and commercial properties such as city parks, soccer fields and sports fields can get up to $5,000. Eligibility and the application are set by LCRA; we'll walk you through what you need from us, like the invoice and proof of completion.

What's in your compost?

Manure-based compost with no bio-solids, screened so there are no sticks, rocks, glass or plastic. Our blends include coffee grounds for slow-release nitrogen.
